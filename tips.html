LEARN & GROW

Simple gardening tips

Practical habits that help plants stay healthy.

01

Understand the light

Place each plant where its light requirements are met. Bright indirect light works well for many popular indoor plants.

02

Don't overwater

Check the soil before watering. Many indoor plants prefer the top layer of soil to dry slightly between waterings.

03

Choose good soil

Use a potting mix suited to the plant and always choose containers with drainage holes.

04

Clean the leaves

Gently wipe dusty leaves from time to time so plants can receive light more effectively.

05

Watch for changes

Yellow leaves, drooping or slow growth can be useful clues that a plant's environment needs attention.

06

Be consistent

Plants respond well to stable routines. Observe first, then adjust watering, light or feeding gradually.
